## 🎵 Audio & Sound Design
- **Engine:** Built with the browser's native **Web Audio API**.
- **Synthesizer Implementation:** 100% procedural sound effects and dynamic ambient music (zero external paid or copyrighted sample packs).
  - Ambient musical modes: Procedural chords (D Minor, A Minor, C Minor, E Major).
  - SFX: Procedural frequency-modulated oscillators and bandpass-filtered noise (footsteps, flares, mirror rotation clicks, electric relays, glass freeze chimes).
